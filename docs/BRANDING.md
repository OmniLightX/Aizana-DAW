# Use of the Aizana name and logo

[Deutsch](BRANDING.de.md)

Aizana welcomes community development. To the extent that the project holds the
relevant rights, the name and logo may be used free of charge for modified
versions, including commercially distributed versions. This permission covers
the project logo (`Resources/AizanaIcon.png`) and banner
(`Resources/brand/aizana-banner.png`) for the purposes described below.

The following conditions apply:

- **Identify the origin:** Refer to the original project,
  [Aizana by Aizanagi](https://github.com/OmniLightX/Aizana-DAW).
- **Identify your version:** Clearly label modified releases as independent
  developments, for example “Aizana – Community version by [name]”.
- **Identify those responsible:** Clearly state who develops, publishes and
  maintains the respective version.
- **Do not imply official approval:** Claim collaboration, review or support by
  the original Aizana project only when it actually exists.
- **Identify logo changes:** Do not present an adapted logo as the official logo
  of the original project.

This permission concerns use of the name, logo and banner to identify and present
Aizana and its derivatives. The software and its dependencies remain subject to
their respective licenses. Existing copyright and license notices must be
preserved as required by those licenses. See the [project license](../LICENSE.md)
and [third-party notices](../THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md).

Contributions to the original project are welcome but are not a condition of
this permission. Developing a derivative under your own name and logo remains
possible under the software licenses.
